`godzilla.glb` is the unmodified file from Poly Pizza
(`https://static.poly.pizza/1c1ae302-b948-4de4-961a-44f762b722cf.glb`, 404 176 bytes). It is
a rigged low-poly dinosaur with 14 animations (the visualization uses
`CharacterArmature|Walk`, `|Idle` and `|Punch`). The dashboard recolours its
`Dino_Main` / `Dino_Secondary` materials at runtime and scales it to look like a kaiju; it
is a generic CC0 dinosaur, not a likeness of Toho's Godzilla.
